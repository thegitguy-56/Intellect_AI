US-20230104859-A1

A system and method for distributed quantum key distribution across heterogeneous network topologies. The present invention, assigned to QuantumNet Industries LLC, relates generally to cryptographic protocols and specifically to dynamic entanglement routing.

As detailed by Dr. Aris Thorne in his foundational paper published on October 14, 2021, traditional repeater networks suffer from decoherence scaling issues. The proposed architecture introduces a novel quantum memory buffer utilizing NV centers in diamond lattices.

In one embodiment, the control plane orchestrates entanglement swapping operations. The central orchestrator, deployed at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) facility in Boulder, Colorado, continuously monitors the fidelity of established links. If the fidelity drops below the threshold established on March 2, 2022, the link is aggressively pruned.

Furthermore, the entanglement generation rate is significantly improved by multiplexing spectral channels. Elena Rostova previously demonstrated a two-fold increase using similar techniques, but this disclosure integrates it directly with the aforementioned memory buffer structure.
