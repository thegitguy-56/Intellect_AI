Method and apparatus for quantum-state error correction in distributed neural processing

Assignee: NeuroQuant Systems LLC | Filed: 2021-11-04

Novelty Score

summarize Executive Summary

This patent describes a novel approach to resolving decoherence issues in distributed quantum neural networks. By implementing a decentralized error-correction protocol across nodes, the system achieves a 40% reduction in state-loss during complex computational tasks. The methodology heavily relies on continuous entangled-state monitoring without collapsing the wave function, presenting a significant leap over prior art which required periodic halting for classical parity checks.

description Abstract

A system and method for mitigating quantum decoherence in distributed topologies. The apparatus comprises a plurality of quantum processing nodes interconnected via entangled quantum channels. A distributed error correction module operates autonomously within each node, utilizing non-demolition measurements to identify parity errors. Upon detection, local unitary transformations are applied to restore the logical qubit state without global system interruption, thereby facilitating uninterrupted neural network training operations in quantum environments.
